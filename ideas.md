# Reference-Grounded Design Brief

## Ground-Truth Reference

The user provided `https://rk-rithik.github.io/` as the visual and structural reference. Fidelity to its **portfolio-first feeling, calm one-page flow, simple navigation, profile-led hero, concise information panels, practical skills presentation, and direct contact ending** takes priority over the previously implemented Minimal and Dark Frame concepts.

## Reference Blueprint

The reference opens with a clean single-line navigation and a wide, light hero. Its hero is organized as a personal introduction on the left and a compact profile-summary card on the right. The design establishes an approachable cybersecurity student identity through personal information, rather than an abstract design system.

The page follows a direct vertical portfolio sequence: About and Education, Skills, Areas of Interest, Featured Project, Courses and Continuous Learning, Beyond Academics, Goals, and Contact. Sections use wide space, moderate type scale, unobtrusive labels, and compact bordered panels. The work is presented plainly, with practical copy and clear status, not as a product catalog.

## Adaptation for Avinash K

Avinash’s revision will use the same portfolio behavior but will not copy the reference’s content, personal photo, or identity. It will use a warm off-white page, charcoal text, and terracotta as the three-colour system. The hero will feature Avinash’s initials / field-seal avatar until a real portrait is supplied, a clear intro, two direct action buttons, and a profile-summary card. Navigation will carry the expected portfolio sections.

The visual treatment will remain original: a more refined field-seal avatar, a subtle monogram instead of the reference portrait, and Yapari restricted to Avinash’s name. Information sections will mirror the reference’s clean structure while presenting only Avinash’s own verified academic details and clearly titled study areas.

## Portfolio Voice

The site should sound like a serious student portfolio: direct, modest, and learning-oriented. It should say what Avinash studies, what he is exploring, and the kind of work he is building, without marketing language or generic placeholder prompts.
