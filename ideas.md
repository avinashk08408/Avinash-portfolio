# Minimal-Classic Portfolio Direction

## Chosen Theme: Quiet Scholar

Quiet Scholar is a restrained personal portfolio direction inspired by **classic academic correspondence and well-made professional documents**. It should feel composed, clear, and credible rather than highly designed or trend-driven. The interface uses conventional spacing, familiar section order, generous breathing room, and simple visual hierarchy.

## Core Principles

The portfolio will be intentionally quiet. Large decorative gestures, oversized type, complex card grids, multiple visual systems, and experimental framing will be removed. The page should let Avinash’s academic identity, current skills, project work, and learning journey carry the visual weight.

## Single Theme System

The full page uses **Ivory Paper `#FAF8F2`** as its one continuous background. **Ink `#1F211F`** is used for all primary text, rules, and buttons. **Sienna `#8D5A44`** is the only accent; it is reserved for short labels, active navigation, small dividers, and selected key words. This retains warmth but is visibly different from previous themes and references.

> **60 / 30 / 10 rule:** Ivory Paper establishes the field, Ink carries all meaningful reading and structure, and Sienna appears only where the eye needs direction.

## Layout Paradigm

The design uses a centered reading column with a compact top navigation, a two-column hero, and ordinary professional section transitions. Each portfolio section is introduced by a small numbered label and a readable heading. Content uses simple lists, slim rules, and modest panels only where they clarify information. The flow follows the exact requested order: Hero, About, Skills, Projects, Education and Journey, Certification, Resume, Activity, Contact, and Footer.

## Typography System

**Libre Baskerville** provides classic section headings. **Manrope** is the body and interface font. **Yapari** is used only for Avinash K’s name in the hero, preserving it as a small personal signature rather than a graphic device. Headings are medium-sized and sentence-like; supporting text remains readable at 14–16px.

## Interaction and Motion

Interactions are low-key: simple underline changes, soft color shifts, and a brief 160–200ms movement on buttons. There are no scroll theatrics, glossy effects, or attention-grabbing decorative animations. The portfolio should feel stable and thoughtful.

## Brand Voice

The writing is direct and modest. It describes learning with confidence but without exaggerated claims. Example lines: “I’m building practical understanding through focused study and small technical projects.” “Open to internships, collaboration, and work that helps me learn by doing.”

## Style Decisions

- Yapari is limited to a small personal signature in the hero and does not serve as a poster-scale masthead.
- Section titles are document-sized and use spacing, rules, and content order for hierarchy rather than editorial drama.
- The AK mark functions as a small personal seal in the header and profile note, never as a technology-product logo.
- Contact closes as a calm professional correspondence section, with a readable note form and no visual placeholder links.

## Required Content Specification

The user has designated `https://avinashk08408.github.io/aviportfolio.github.io/` as the required source for the **exact portfolio structure and content**. Its biography, project titles and descriptions, education records, public contact details, opportunity statement, and the “Coming Soon” project item must remain intact. The Quiet Scholar revision may change only presentation, spacing, typography, and theme; it must not rewrite the source content into a different professional voice, remove the fourth project, or expand the source’s compact navigation into additional sections.
